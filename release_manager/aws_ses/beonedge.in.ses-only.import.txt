;; ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
;; WHY THIS FILE EXISTS SEPARATELY
;;
;; Cloudflare's import is ADDITIVE — it creates records and does not overwrite
;; existing ones. Importing a full zone that already matches your account would
;; attempt to re-create all 14 records you have. For most types that is merely
;; noise, but two of them fail dangerously:
;;
;;   - a second apex "v=spf1" TXT record makes SPF a permanent error; a domain
;;     may publish exactly one;
;;   - a second _dmarc TXT record makes DMARC not apply at all (RFC 7489
;;     §6.6.3: policy discovery halts when it finds more than one record).
;;
;; This file therefore contains only what is genuinely new. Nothing here
;; collides with an existing record name, so the import cannot duplicate
;; anything.
;;
;; ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
;; TWO CORRECTIONS TO THE AWS CSV
;;
;; 1. AWS's "TXT _dmarc.beonedge.in  v=DMARC1; p=none;" is OMITTED. That is
;;    AWS's starter policy for domains with no DMARC. You already publish a
;;    stricter one (p=quarantine, with rua/ruf reporting) that is already
;;    correct for SES: adkim=r aligns with SES Easy DKIM signing as
;;    d=beonedge.in, and aspf=r aligns with the custom MAIL FROM subdomain.
;;    Adding AWS's would either duplicate the record (breaking DMARC entirely)
;;    or downgrade your policy and lose the reporting addresses.
;;
;; 2. Trailing periods added to all CNAME and MX targets. The CSV omits them.
;;    Cloudflare requires fully-qualified content for CNAME/MX/NS/PTR/SRV in an
;;    imported zone file; without the final "." the target is read as relative
;;    to the origin and becomes e.g. "...amazonses.com.beonedge.in".
;;
;; ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

;;   Dashboard: DNS > Records > Import and Export > Import DNS records.
;;   The "Proxy imported DNS records" checkbox does not matter here — each CNAME
;;   below carries an explicit cf-proxied:false tag, and Cloudflare gives that
;;   tag precedence over the checkbox.

;;   API:
;;     curl "https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/zones/$ZONE_ID/dns_records/import" \
;;       --request POST \
;;       --header "Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN" \
;;       --form "file=@beonedge.in.ses-only.import.txt"

;; ── SES custom MAIL FROM: support.beonedge.in ────────────────────────────────
;; A SEPARATE DNS namespace from the support@beonedge.in mailbox. That address is
;; routed by the apex MX records (Zoho) and is untouched by these two lines.
;; The MX receives asynchronous bounce notifications; the TXT authorises SES for
;; the envelope sender domain, which is what gives SPF alignment under aspf=r.
support.beonedge.in.	300	IN	MX	10 feedback-smtp.ap-south-1.amazonses.com.
support.beonedge.in.	300	IN	TXT	"v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all"
