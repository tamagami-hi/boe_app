# UI audit U3: admin app, phone

Read-only audit of the admin console at phone width (the admin APK's surface), for the UI overhaul (DEC-40). It keeps today's BeOnEdge identity and design system (DEC-45). Fixes wait for the per-surface plan (U5) and its approval (DEC-47).

## Summary

On a phone the admin console is a desktop console squeezed into 390 px. Seventeen screens are wide tables (up to ten columns, 672 to 1,117 px) inside a 346 px scroll box, with columns cut in the middle of names and amounts (UAM-03). No screen has a back control, 23 detail screens highlight nothing in the bottom bar, and the page title is printed twice, differently on 13 screens (UAM-01, UAM-02). Long filter-chip walls push lists below the first screen (UAM-04). The words assume an engineer is reading ("append-only ledger", "basis points", raw reason codes) (UAM-05). Destructive actions sit beside navigation and repeat down the ledger in red (UAM-06). The shared problems of contrast, disabled buttons and dates appear here too. Failed reads are handled well: every tested admin screen shows a failure with "Try again" (no endless skeleton).

Counts: 29 findings: 0 high, 12 medium, 17 low.

## Method

- **Commit audited:** `a842496` on `track/u-audits`; Phase I screens (nominee requests, SIP early exit) are not on this branch and are left out (DEC-47).
- **Width:** 390×844 CSS px, Chromium 1248 through Playwright 1.59.1, `isMobile` and touch on, device scale 1, `en-IN`, `Asia/Kolkata`, reduced motion on. A browser at phone width, not an emulator or APK (DEC-47).
- **Stack and data:** local only, as in U1 ([audit-client-mobile.md](audit-client-mobile.md), Method): throwaway PostgreSQL and Mailpit, migrations 001 to 071, seeds, the backend and the admin dev server in the Node 22 container, payments unconfigured. Signed in as the seeded superadmin, so every permission-gated screen was visible. Data: three funds, five clients (recordings, an admin SIP schedule, value updates, two maturity dates, one with options sent, a nominee, two support requests, two reviews, an unused invite), FAQs and an admin email template, all created through the admin API and the client app. The launch maturity prompt was captured once and dismissed for the other screens.
- **Per route:** viewport and full-page screenshots and the in-page probe of U1 (headings, landmarks, shell, buttons, targets, overflow, clipped text, forms, states, money, dates, copy, type sizes, contrast). Detail routes with no local record (an application, a fund receipt, a mandate) were opened with an unknown id to see the not-found state. Screenshots and JSON are in the closed track's scratch folder `.temp/closed/u-audits/` (not committed); paths below are relative to it.
- **States:** failed (500) and slow (7 s) reads on 16 admin screens, through Playwright route interception of `GET /api/v1/admin/*` (the session read excluded).
- **Keyboard:** tab order on sign-in and Overview at 390 px.
- **Not checked:** the APK and the Android back button; roles other than superadmin (support and finance views, DEC-36); a real screen reader; the Phase I screens; contrast over gradients.

Severity: **high** breaks a screen or blocks a task; **medium** is hard to use or breaks a rule in `RULES.md`; **low** is polish.

## Inconsistencies

### Between routes (phone)

- **Back control:** none on any route (UAM-01). Two in-page "Back to users" and "Go back" buttons exist in code (after an erase, inside maturity dialogs) but none showed on the audited screens.
- **Current item in the bottom bar:** the bar's own item on Overview, Users, Funds and Client values; "More" on 18 routes; nothing on 23 detail and sub-pages (UAM-01, UAM-13).
- **Page title:** in the app bar and again as the page heading on every route; different on 13 routes (UAM-02).
- **Section chips:** shown on 17 routes, not on the other pages of the same sections (UAM-12).
- **Container width:** wide (1,280 px cap) on 38 routes, content (960 px) on 6 (create a client, the Client values hub, individual adjustment, fund receipt, App config, not found), form (560 px) on Erase client. At 390 px these differ only in how far tables can grow.
- **Header actions:** five styles of the same "create" action (UAM-21).
- **Lists:** tables on 17 routes, cards on Funds, Applications, Client values hub, the Today tiles and the review and ticket details (UAM-03).
- **Destructive actions:** red danger buttons inline (Reverse, Suspend, Close, Archive), a secondary pill (Erase client, Archive template) and a primary-tone "Remove the nominee" (UAM-06).
- **Not found:** a red error panel with "Try again" on application, receipt and mandate details; the route-level not-found page is neutral (UAM-18).
- **Money:** paise everywhere in admin; inputs ungrouped (UAM-15).
- **Filters:** chip rows on Applications, Users, Funds, Support, Reviews, Emails, Email templates, Audit; a select on Monthly payments and Monthly value update; text inputs and date inputs on Audit.

### Phone and web layouts of the same route

Measured at 390 px against 1440 px (`scripts/phonevsweb.py`):

- The app bar (title, Sign out) becomes a page with no bar title and a small "Sign out" at the top right; the bottom bar and section chips give way to the 22-entry sidebar.
- Tables are the same component at both widths; on the phone they scroll sideways in a 346 px box.
- Header actions sit under the title on the phone and at its right on the web.
- Primary buttons are 44 and 52 px on the phone, 40 and 48 px on the web; header and row actions drop to 36 px.
- The Client values hub's four task cards stack on the phone and form a two-column grid on the web.
- No route changes its heading, its back behaviour or its primary action between phone and web.

## Pattern inventory (phone)

- **Buttons:** primary (dark pill), secondary (quiet pill, often with a trailing arrow chip), ghost (text pill), danger (red gradient pill, 31 on the audited phone routes), and plain text links in tables. Heights 44 px (most), 52 px (fund forms, fund size), 20 px for links in table rows (UAM-22). Active filter chips and section chips use the primary dark pill (UAM-04).
- **Cards and lists:** card grids for funds, applications, Today tiles and the Client values task cards; tables for everything else (UAM-03); label–value rows in cards for details.
- **Forms:** labels above fields in 12 px uppercase with a red asterisk, hints under the field. Submit placement varies: full width under the fields (create a client, adjustments, template editor), left-aligned and content-sized (fund creation, holdings, support reply, review handling), centred (fund size). Only four admin feature files use the shared `Form` (UAM-09). Recording an investment uses a review-then-confirm dialog.
- **Loading:** skeletons in the shape of the content, no busy state (UAM-25).
- **Empty:** the shared centred panel ("Nothing waiting here", "No contact changes").
- **Error:** the shared red panel with "Try again" and a reference on every tested screen; inline read failures inside cards.
- **Money:** ₹ with Indian grouping, always with paise; signed growth in green; "Investments are subject to market risk." appears only where the client's own portfolio summary is shown.
- **Dates:** "16 Nov 2026", "16 Nov 2026, 17:52", "Nov 2026", "Sept", relative "In 24 days" and "Today", native inputs in "dd/mm/yyyy" (UAM-17, UAM-24).
- **Toasts and dialogs:** confirmation dialogs (`ConfirmDialog`, `Modal` on the `Sheet` primitive) for recording, maturity actions, template sends and lifecycle changes; bottom sheets on the phone. Toasts on fund creation, fund workspace and fund size changes; inline alerts elsewhere; the Monthly payments sheet uses an inline "Recorded / Record another" result.
- **Type and spacing:** the same two families plus JetBrains Mono for slugs and references; twelve sizes on the phone, 391 nodes at 10 px (UAM-23). Page padding 16 px, page top 32 px, section gap 32 px.
