# UI audit U1: client app, phone

Read-only audit of the client app at phone width, for the UI overhaul (DEC-40). It keeps today's BeOnEdge identity and design system (DEC-45) and proposes only refinements within it. Fixes wait for the per-surface plan (U5) and its approval (DEC-47).

## Summary

The client app is visually consistent: one shell, one card style, one set of type faces, Indian rupee grouping everywhere, and a market-risk footnote on most money screens. Most failed reads show a clear failure with a retry. The problems are in four places:

- **Failure handling on Home and Profile.** When the eligibility read fails, Home and Profile never leave their skeletons and "Checking…" placeholders, and the app re-requests every Home read about 78 times a minute (UCM-01). This breaks rule 4.
- **Navigation and the page shell.** The app bar never names the page, the four top-level tabs carry a back arrow that Home does not, 22 sub-pages highlight no tab, and back goes to a fixed parent rather than where the client came from (UCM-02 to UCM-04).
- **Words that do not match how money moves.** Until `featureFlags.onlinePayments=false` is published (G11), empty states and Activity talk about payments the client never makes online (UCM-05).
- **Accessibility details.** The faint text token used for every market-risk line, timestamp and reference is 2.6 to 3.1:1 against its backgrounds. Disabled primary buttons look enabled. Ten-pixel text is common (UCM-08 to UCM-10).

Counts: 30 findings: 1 high, 11 medium, 18 low.

## Method

- **Commit audited:** `a842496` on `track/u-audits` (Phase I screens are not on this branch and are left out, per DEC-47).
- **Widths:** 360×800 and 390×844 CSS px, Chromium 1248 through Playwright 1.59.1, `isMobile` and touch on, device scale 1, `en-IN`, `Asia/Kolkata`, reduced motion on so the reveal animation does not hide content in screenshots. A browser at phone width, not an emulator or APK (DEC-47).
- **Stack:** local only. Throwaway PostgreSQL and Mailpit from `test_e2e/local-stack.sh`'s docker steps, migrations 001 to 071, `seed:dev`, `seed:auth:dev`, the backend and the client dev server inside the Node 22 container. Payments unconfigured (DEC-14, DEC-22). The published app config was empty, so the app ran with `featureFlags.onlinePayments` unset, as production does until G11 is published. A second pass faked `onlinePayments=false` in the browser (no server change) to see the payments-off copy.
- **Data:** created through the admin API and the client app on this stack: three funds (two published, one draft), five clients. The main client has an earlier ₹2,50,000 recording, an admin SIP schedule with six monthly recordings, two value updates, a maturity due on 10 Dec 2026 with options sent, a nominee, a support request and a review. Others cover an unverified client, a client with nothing recorded (empty states) and an unused invite.
- **Per route and width:** a viewport and a full-page screenshot, plus an in-page probe that records headings, landmarks, the shell (app bar, back control, bottom bar), buttons with tone and size, touch targets, overflow, clipped text, form label placement, loading, empty and error components, money strings, date strings, banned words, type sizes and computed contrast. Screenshots and the raw JSON are in the track's scratch folder `.temp/u-audits/` (not committed); paths below are relative to it.
- **States:** for 13 money and account screens, every `GET /api/v1/client/*` was answered with a 500 (failed read) and, separately, delayed 7 s (slow read), through Playwright route interception.
- **Keyboard:** tab order and the focus indicator on sign-in and Home at 390 px.
- **Code:** each finding names the source line where the cause is clear. The parallel static lanes (`u-static-client`, `u-static-admin`) inventory the code side.
- **Not checked:** the APK itself, the Android back button and native plugins (biometric lock, secure storage, update sheet); a real screen reader; the Phase I screens; contrast over gradients and images (the probe skips text whose background is a gradient, about 60% of text nodes); right-to-left and very large system font sizes; print output of the statement.

Severity: **high** breaks a screen or blocks a task; **medium** is hard to use or breaks a rule in `RULES.md`; **low** is polish.

## Inconsistencies

### Between routes (phone)

- **Back arrow:** shown on every route except Home, including the four tab roots (Funds, Portfolio, Activity, Profile), where it leads to Home. It targets the manifest parent, not the previous screen (UCM-03, UCM-04).
- **Current tab:** marked on the five tab roots only; 22 sub-pages mark none (UCM-03).
- **Page title:** never in the app bar; the page heading is the only title. Six headings differ from the manifest title that native back and future tab titles would use: Home ("Good to see you, …"), a fund (its name, manifest "Fund"), an investment (its fund name, manifest "Your investment"), maturity options ("Your maturity options"), not found ("We could not find that page"), and the blocked route.
- **Container width:** 33 routes use the content width (960 px cap), 7 use the form width (560 px): email verification, verification status, security and the four parked payment screens, so Contact details and Security, two sibling profile pages, have different widths. The statement print page uses the wide container.
- **Header actions:** "Receipts" (Activity) and "Print statement" (Statements) sit between heading and description; "Value updates" sits in a section header; "See all" is a text link with an arrow chip; "Fund details" and "All receipts" are pills at the end of content (UCM-20, UCM-23).
- **Card links:** underlined gold text, whole-card links and pills (UCM-21).
- **Empty states:** same panel everywhere; actions with and without arrows, left-aligned in a centred panel; footnote present on some empty money screens and not others (UCM-11, UCM-22).
- **Not found:** route not found is a page heading, a card and "Go to home"; a missing SIP plan is a plain card with "Back to SIP plans"; a missing payment is the parked payment screen's own message.
- **Money:** whole rupees in lists and summaries, paise on maturity options and receipt detail; zero amounts green on Home and statements (UCM-15, UCM-16).
- **Status words:** "Available", "Unlocked" and "Locked" for investing; "Verified", "In progress", "Pending" for email; "As at" and "As of" (UCM-18).
- **Auth pages:** eyebrow "CLIENT ACCESS" on sign-in, "BEONEDGE" on the password pages (UCM-28).

### Phone and web layouts of the same route

Measured at 390 px against 1280 px (`scripts/phonevsweb.py`):

- 35 routes lose the app-bar back arrow at web width; only the parked SIP plan page keeps a way back, its in-page "Back to SIP plans" (U2, UCW-02).
- The app bar (back, bell) becomes a floating pill bar (wordmark, five tabs, bell); the bottom bar disappears.
- Funds: cards on the phone, a four-column table on the web.
- Fund page: the two invest buttons are stacked full-width on the phone and side by side on the web; the risk badge moves from under the title to the right of it.
- Detail rows: label left, value right-aligned on the phone; a fixed 14 rem label column and a left-aligned value, capped at 44 rem, on the web.
- Header actions move from under the heading to its right.
- Primary buttons are 52 px (auth and verification forms) and 44 px on the phone, 48 px and 40 px on the web; header and table actions drop to 36 px.
- Page padding 16 px (phone), 24 px (768 px), 32 px (1280 px and up); page top 32, 40 and 64 px; section gap 32, 40 and 48 px. These come from the layout recipe and are consistent.

## Pattern inventory (phone)

- **Buttons:** four tones in use: primary (dark gradient pill), secondary (quiet pill, usually with a trailing arrow chip), ghost (text-only pill: "Forgot your password?", "Mark read", app-bar icons) and text links styled in the brand gold. Heights: 52 px on auth and verification forms, 44 px elsewhere, 36 px for text links. No danger tone is used in the client app; the only destructive action, "Sign out" at the end of Profile, is a secondary button. The maturity choice preselects withdrawal (UCM-07).
- **Cards and lists:** every list is stacked cards (receipts, value updates, activity, notifications, funds, positions); detail screens use label–value rows inside one card. The only table on the phone is the statement print page, in a scroll box (UCM-25).
- **Forms:** labels above the field in 12 px uppercase with a red asterisk for required fields, hint text under the field, and the primary submit full width under the fields; a secondary action stacks under it (verification). All client forms use the shared `Form`.
- **Loading:** skeleton cards shaped like the content, appearing at once, with no busy state for assistive technology (UCM-29). Home and Profile use "Checking…" text for statuses.
- **Empty:** a centred panel with a serif title, one sentence and sometimes an action.
- **Error:** a red-tinted panel "Something went wrong", "This is on our side, not yours. Please try again in a moment.", "Try again" and a short reference. Shown within 8 to 15 s on 11 of 13 tested screens; not on Home or Profile (UCM-01). Inline read failures use `InlineReadFailure`.
- **Money:** ₹ with Indian grouping on every amount found (no grouping errors on 45 routes), signed growth with "+" in green; the footnote "Values recorded by BeOnEdge · Last updated … · Investments are subject to market risk." in 12 px faint text at the end of money screens.
- **Dates:** "05 Nov 2026" (two-digit day, `en-IN`), "16 Nov 2026, 17:52" (24-hour), "Nov 2026" for months, "Sept" for September, "10 December 2026" in server-written notification text, "9:00 AM – 6:00 PM" in published support hours, "today" in one relative label (UCM-17).
- **Toasts and dialogs:** no dialog opened on the audited client routes; feedback is inline alerts above or inside the form ("Request sent", "Review received"). Toasts are used only by Notifications and the legal contact copy action.
- **Type and spacing:** two families (Fraunces for headings, Instrument Sans for text) plus JetBrains Mono for references. Thirteen font sizes on the phone: 10, 12, 13, 15, 16, 18, 22, 32 to 35 (fluid page titles) and 43 to 47 px (fluid display). 586 text nodes at 10 px (UCM-10).
